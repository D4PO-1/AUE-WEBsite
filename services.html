Our Professional Practice Areas

Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. (Chartered Accountants) renders high-level statutory, financial, tax, and managerial advisory services tailored to public and corporate institutions.

STATUTORY AUDIT

Our firm gives audit opinions on the financial statements of companies, government corporations, and parastatal organizations in accordance with the Companies and Allied Matters Act and other enabling Enactments.

In the course of our statutory audit, Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. understudies the organizational, financial, and operational controls of our clients, highlights any weaknesses that come to our notice, and makes actionable recommendations to improve performance and Return on Investment (ROI).

MANAGEMENT AUDIT

We carry out operational and value-for-money audits by reviewing policy performance and management systems to ensure economy and efficiency. We assist clients in ascertaining whether shareholder directives are being implemented and whether the company is effectively run without short-term biases compromising long-term success.

At the conclusion of the audit, we report directly to Shareholders or the Audit Committee with targeted performance adjustments. Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. has extensive experience undertaking Management Audits on Joint Venture projects involving Foreign Finance.

INVESTIGATION

We undertake specialized inquiries and independent reporting for tailored financial scenarios, including:

  • Prospectus Reports: Expressing independent reporting accountant opinions on prospectus financial statements for public share/debenture offerings.
  • Profits Forecast: Expressing professional opinions on profit forecasts during share issues, takeovers, or mergers.
  • Acquisition of Companies & Purchase of Business.
  • Prospective Lending and Investment Evaluation.
  • Partnership Arrangements & Forensic Fraud Inquiries.

MERGERS AND ACQUISITIONS

Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. assists corporate entities across all accounting and financial aspects of Mergers and Acquisitions. We provide continuous evaluation reports and valuation metrics to ensure intended operational and financial synergies are fully realized.

TAXATION CONSULTANCY

We act as lead advisors on comprehensive taxation matters covering corporate and individual tax affairs. Our core tax solutions include tax computations, statutory filing of returns with Federal and State Revenue Authorities, and obtaining Tax Clearance Certificates (TCC).

RECEIVERSHIP AND LIQUIDATIONS

Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. acts as Receivers and Managers regarding the dissolution of Corporate and Non-Corporate bodies under Voluntary or Compulsory winding up in strict accordance with the Companies and Allied Matters Act and other enabling enactments.

MANAGEMENT CONSULTANCY

Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. provides end-to-end specialized management consulting across the following capabilities:

  • Designing and Installation of Accounting Systems
  • Company Secretaryship Services
  • Business Permit & Expatriate Quota Advisory
  • Technical Service Contracts & Agency Arrangements
  • Human Resources Management – Recruitment & Development
  • Incorporation of Limited & Unlimited Liability Companies
  • Cash Flow Projections, Planning, and Control Systems
  • Feasibility Studies & Business Plan Development
  • Business Environment Information & Strategic Intelligence
  • Industry Cameos and Performance Comparisons
  • Data Processing & Computerization of Accounting Systems
  • Management Accounts & Information Systems (MIS) Design
  • Budgeting & Budgetary Control Systems
  • Cost Management & Financial Architecture Design
