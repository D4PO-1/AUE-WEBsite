About Us

At Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. we provide bespoke accounting, audit and sundry actuarial services to discerning clients.

Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. employs a crop of well seasoned and highly experienced professionals dedicated to excellence in their individual approach to providing solutions to varied accountancy and management problems. Building on our five decades of stability, we continually integrate modern technology and refined analytical tools into our advisory framework. This enables our experts to address increasingly dynamic statutory demands, modern tax regulations, and complex financial risk models with speed and rigorous precision. Driven by a culture of integrity and discretion, we view every engagement as a long-term strategic partnership. Beyond standard compliance, our goal is to safeguard institutional value, uncover operational efficiencies, and empower our clients to make sound, forward-looking decisions in an evolving economy.
