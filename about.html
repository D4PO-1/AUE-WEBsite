About Us

At Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. we provide bespoke accounting, audit and sundry actuarial services to discerning clients.

Anjous, Uku, Eweka & Co. employs a crop of well seasoned and highly experienced professionals dedicated to excellence in their individual approach to providing solutions to varied accountancy and management problems.

Building on our five decades of stability, we continually integrate modern technology and refined analytical tools into our advisory framework. This enables our experts to address increasingly dynamic statutory demands, modern tax regulations, and complex financial risk models with speed and rigorous precision.

Driven by a culture of integrity and discretion, we view every engagement as a long-term strategic partnership. Beyond standard compliance, our goal is to safeguard institutional value, uncover operational efficiencies, and empower our clients to make sound, forward-looking decisions in an evolving economy.

Our Vision

To be the premier accounting and business advisory firm recognized for absolute integrity, financial clarity, and transformational value creation across industries.

Our Mission

Deliver rigorous, compliant, and bespoke financial, tax, and audit services that safeguard corporate assets, optimize performance, and drive sustainable growth.

Our Objective

Empower our clients through proactive financial risk management, seamless statutory reporting, and strategic tax strategies tailored to complex corporate landscapes.
